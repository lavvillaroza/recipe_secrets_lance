Our Story

Two kitchens, one family, and twenty years of recipes worth keeping secret.

Since 2014

Started in a food truck, built on family recipes

Recipe Secrets began as a weekend food truck parked outside Portland farmers' markets, serving the Pan family's recipes to anyone curious enough to ask what was inside the braise. Twelve years later, that same curiosity fills a 90-seat dining room. We still cook from handwritten recipe cards, still buy from the same produce stand that gave us credit when we couldn't afford groceries, and still believe the best meals come from recipes passed down, not looked up.

Meet the Chef

The person behind the pass

Chef Elena Park

Executive Chef & Co-Owner

Elena trained in Osaka and Seoul before returning to Portland to open Recipe Secrets with her brother, Daniel. Her cooking leans on fermentation, patience, and a refusal to over-plate anything. When she's not in the kitchen, she's usually at the farmers' market arguing about radishes.

Our Philosophy

Ingredients first, technique second

We source from Pacific Northwest farms and fisheries whenever possible, ferment our own condiments in-house, and change the menu with the seasons instead of forcing the seasons to fit the menu. Nothing on your plate is there by accident — every garnish, sauce and pairing is tested a dozen times before it earns a spot.

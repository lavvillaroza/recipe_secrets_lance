Welcome

Cooking that remembers where it came from

At Recipe Secrets, we blend the techniques of classic Japanese, Korean and Sichuan kitchens with Pacific Northwest produce. Every dish starts with a family recipe and ends somewhere new — built for sharing, best enjoyed slowly, with people you like.

Chef's Omakase Selection

A rotating five-piece nigiri set sourced from the day's best catch, finished with yuzu salt.
